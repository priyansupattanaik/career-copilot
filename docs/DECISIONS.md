# Design Decision Log

| # | Date | Decision | Principle | Rationale in this context | Alternative rejected & why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-14 | Accent warm to cold (`#6EA8FF`) | Exactly one accent per ux-psych-design Phase 3 + WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (8.71:1 against OLED black canvas, 7.18:1 on surface-2, 6.26:1 on surface-3) | A calm, cold palette aligns with the product's professional, focused atmosphere on OLED True Black with Dark Midnight Mesh. High contrast exceeds AA standard on every surface step and interactive element. | Keeping the orange accent (`#FF7A45`): Warm tone competed with warning states and created visual heat instead of a calm, focused environment. |
| 2 | 2026-10-14 | Secondary text token adjusted from `#A3B1C2` to `#A5B3C5` | WCAG 2.2 AA Contrast (>= 7:1 for secondary text on elevated surfaces) | On `--surface-3` (`#1C2735`), `#A3B1C2` yielded 6.92:1 (0.08 below target). Raising to `#A5B3C5` ensures 7.08:1 on surface-3 and 9.85:1 on canvas, strictly meeting the >= 7.0:1 standard while preserving cold slate hue. | Keeping `#A3B1C2`: Failed mathematical threshold on surface-3. |
| 3 | 2026-10-14 | Control boundary token `--border-control` (`#66788F`) | WCAG 2.2 Non-text Contrast (>= 3:1 against background) | Control boundaries (inputs, switches, segmented controls, radio cards) achieve 4.22:1 on surface-1, 3.83:1 on surface-2, and 3.34:1 on surface-3, making affordances immediately perceptible without visual clutter. | Using `--hairline` (`rgba(148,163,184,0.18)`): Border had insufficient non-text contrast for active input boundaries. |
